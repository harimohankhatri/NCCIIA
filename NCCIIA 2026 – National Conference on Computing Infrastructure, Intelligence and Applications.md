## Computing that works for Nepal

One day in Bhaktapur for researchers, teachers, engineers and students to share what is working in cloud, edge, security, AI and the applications that matter to Nepal.

NCCIIA brings academia, government and industry into the same room. Expect keynotes, parallel paper sessions, a panel on Nepal's AI future, and a student project showcase.

We welcome original research papers, review articles, case studies and applied work. Special emphasis goes to agentic and generative AI, edge and cloud computing, trustworthy AI, Nepali-language technologies, and applications in agriculture, disaster management, health, education and governance.

## Six tracks, one conference

Submit to any track. Papers are presented in parallel technical sessions.

Computing Infrastructure and Systems

- Cloud-native architectures, containers, serverless
- Edge and fog computing
- Low-cost GPU, HPC, parallel and distributed computing
- Green and energy-efficient computing
- Networks, 5G/6G, software-defined networking
- IoT, embedded systems, TinyML, FPGA
- Blockchain and decentralized systems

Security and Digital Trust

- Cybersecurity, zero trust, AI threat detection
- Post-quantum cryptography
- Privacy-preserving computing
- Digital forensics and malware analysis
- Secure software development, DevSecOps

Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning

- Agentic AI and multi-agent systems
- Large and small language models
- Generative AI for text, image, audio, code
- Explainable and responsible AI
- Federated learning, reinforcement learning, robotics
- AI ethics, governance and policy

Computer Vision and Language Technologies

- Image processing and computer vision
- Multimodal and vision-language models
- Remote sensing and satellite imagery
- Medical image analysis
- NLP and speech for Nepali and low-resource languages
- OCR for Devanagari script

Data and Software Engineering

- Big data and data engineering pipelines
- Databases, NoSQL, vector databases
- Knowledge graphs and information retrieval
- AI-assisted programming and testing
- Microservices, APIs, mobile and web apps
- Open-source and digital public goods

AI Applications and Societal Impact

- Agriculture: crop disease and yield prediction
- Wildfire, flood and landslide monitoring
- Health informatics and telemedicine
- E-governance and digital identity
- Education technology, FinTech, smart cities
- HCI, accessibility, AR/VR
